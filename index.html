🏁 The Chessboard & Setup

A standard chessboard is made of 64 squares (32 light, 32 dark). Understanding how to set up the board properly is the very first step in chess!

Inspected Square: e4 (File e, Rank 4)
1

"White on Right" (右下角為白格)

Always orient the board so that each player has a light (white) square in their bottom-right corner (h1 for White, a8 for Black).

2

"Queen on Her Own Color" (后在自家顏色)

The White Queen always begins on a light square (d1). The Black Queen begins on a dark square (d8). The King stands right beside her on e1 / e8.

📐 Coordinates: Files & Ranks

  • Files (列/直行): 8 vertical columns named with letters a, b, c, d, e, f, g, h from left to right (from White's perspective).
  • Ranks (行/橫行): 8 horizontal rows numbered 1 to 8. White starts on ranks 1 & 2; Black starts on ranks 7 & 8.
  • Diagonals (斜行): Straight slant lines of squares sharing the exact same color. Bishops and Queens travel along diagonals!

✨ Three Essential Special Rules

Chess has 3 special maneuvers that every beginner must master: Castling for king safety, Pawn Promotion for endgame power, and En Passant.

King Safety O-O / O-O-O

🏰 Castling (王車易位)

The only move where two pieces move at once! The King steps 2 squares toward the Rook, and the Rook leaps right over the King.

Strict Requirements:
  • Neither King nor chosen Rook has moved yet.
  • All squares between King and Rook are empty.
  • The King is NOT currently in check.
  • The King cannot pass through or land on a square attacked by an enemy piece.
Pawn Superpower =Q, =R, =B, =N

👑 Promotion (兵的升變)

When a humble pawn marches all the way to the far end of the board (rank 8 for White, rank 1 for Black), it immediately transforms into a Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight!

Key Facts:
  • Almost always promoted to a Queen ("Queening") because she is the most powerful piece.
  • You can have multiple Queens on the board at the same time!
  • Underpromotion to a Knight is sometimes used to deliver an immediate check or fork.
French for "In Passing" e.p.

⚡ En Passant (吃過路兵)

A special pawn-capturing rule. If an enemy pawn uses its two-square first move to land directly adjacent to your pawn on the 5th rank, you can capture it diagonally as if it had only moved one square!

One Critical Catch:

You must execute En Passant on the very next turn immediately after the enemy pawn moved. If you wait one turn, the right to capture en passant is permanently lost!

👑 Check, Checkmate, and Draws

The King is never actually captured or removed from the board! The game ends the very moment a King is checkmated, or when a draw is reached.

⚠️

Check (將軍)

The King is under direct attack by an enemy piece. The player in check must get out of check immediately!

Remember CPR: 3 Ways Out!
  • C - Capture: Take the attacking piece.
  • P - Protect / Block: Slide a piece between attacker and King.
  • R - Run: Step the King to an unattacked square.
🏆

Checkmate (將死/將殺)

The ultimate objective in chess! The King is in check, and there is zero legal escape moves (cannot Capture, Protect, or Run).

Game Over Instantly:

The attacker wins immediately (1 point). White win is written as 1 - 0, Black win is written as 0 - 1. Shake hands and congratulate your opponent!

🤝

Stalemate & Draws (逼和與和棋)

A draw (tied game) awards ½ point to each player. The most famous draw is Stalemate!

Stalemate (逼和): A player's King is NOT in check, but they have no legal moves left anywhere.
Insufficient Material: Neither side has enough pieces to checkmate (e.g., King vs King).
Threefold Repetition: Same exact position repeated 3 times.
Draw by Agreement: Both players mutually agree to a draw.
